Eliminating Counterfeits in Ultra-Rare Spirits

The global market for vintage whiskies and investment casks faces unprecedented counterfeiting risks. Whiskey Vault establishes an unbroken chain of custody, locking original distillery fill logs, spectrometer chemical fingerprints, and cask deeds to Ethereum smart contracts.

Every recorded bottle or cask carries a unique cryptographic multihash anchored across decentralized IPFS nodes, ensuring that ownership titles, distillery certificates, and tasting records can never be forged, duplicated, or erased.

The Provenance Verification Lifecycle

Four cryptographic steps ensuring lifetime authenticity for fine spirits

1

Record Submission

Distilleries and collectors submit bottle serials, cask deeds, chemical assays, and high-res certificate documentation.

2

IPFS Pinning

Digital certificates are hashed into a deterministic SHA-256 CID and pinned permanently across decentralized storage nodes.

3

Ethereum Notarization

Smart contract records an immutable, timestamped transaction receipt with author signature and block height.

4

Instant Verification

Prospective buyers, auctioneers, and archivists verify authentic provenance anywhere in seconds via search or transaction ID.
